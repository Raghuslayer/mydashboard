## Tips for Maximum Impact

### 1. Add Immediately
Don't wait! Add achievements right after they happen while the emotion is fresh.

### 2. Be Specific
Instead of "Did well at work" → "Closed $50K deal after 3 months of negotiations"

### 3. Include Feelings
"I felt unstoppable" or "This proved I could do hard things"

### 4. Add Context
What made it hard? What did you overcome? What did you learn?

### 5. Review Regularly
- Morning: Start day with confidence
- Before challenges: Remember your capability
- Evening: Reflect on daily wins
- Tough times: Instant motivation boost

### 6. Mix Big & Small
- Big: "Graduated college"
- Small: "Woke up at 5 AM for 7 days straight"

Both matter. Both prove you can do hard things.
